Good interfaces make the next useful action feel obvious without making every other action disappear.

Start with a clear hierarchy

Focus begins with deciding what matters most. A strong heading, a concise explanation, and one clear action give readers a dependable path through the page.

Use restraint as a feature

Every color, border, and control competes for attention. When the visual system uses emphasis deliberately, important moments stand out naturally.

Make the default useful

Thoughtful defaults reduce effort and uncertainty. They let people begin with confidence while preserving room for more advanced choices.
